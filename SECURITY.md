# Security policy

Primordial Rise takes the security of its players and their accounts seriously.

## Reporting a vulnerability

**Please do not open a public issue for a security problem.**

Report it privately through GitHub's private vulnerability reporting:
[open a private report](https://github.com/Primordial-Rise/.github/security/advisories/new).
Only the maintainers can see it.

Please include:

- what is affected (the browser demo, the Windows or Linux desktop build, an account, or
  the game's online services);
- the build or version, if you know it;
- steps to reproduce, and what an attacker could do with it;
- any proof-of-concept, screenshots or logs (remove any passwords or tokens first).

## What happens next

- We aim to acknowledge a report within **3 working days**.
- We will keep you informed while we investigate and fix it, and tell you when a fix
  ships.
- If you would like credit, we are happy to name you once the fix is out.

## Scope

In scope: the Primordial Rise game clients, its online services and player accounts.

Out of scope: denial-of-service and volume testing, social engineering, physical attacks,
and findings in third-party services we do not run. Please test only against your own
account and never access other players' data.

## Safe harbour

We will not pursue anyone who reports a vulnerability in good faith, follows this policy
and gives us reasonable time to fix it before any disclosure.
